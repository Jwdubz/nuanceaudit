I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way someone researching a facelift, rhinoplasty or eyelid surgery would. The doctors, the long service pages and the patient reviews are all there. What search tools get to read is much thinner, and the load time is long.

What I found

01

Your pages tell Google almost nothing about themselves.

The homepage is titled “Home page” with the description “ss”. Your Surgical page is titled “Service 1” with the description “asdasd”. Of the 46 addresses in your sitemap that load, 44 have an empty title, none has a description, and none has a main heading. In my search for your name, your homepage came back titled “Home page” and your Dr. Khavkin page came back with no title at all.

02

Your sitemap points to dead pages and leaves out your facelift page.

Your sitemap lists 59 addresses. 46 load, 11 send the visitor to your “Not Found” page (for example /service/deep-plane-facelift, /blogs, /abouts and /services, plus /blank and /blankpages, which is listed twice), 1 is a server error page (a blog post), and 1 redirects to a working page. The page your menu uses for the Deep Plane Facelift, /service/facelift, isn't in the sitemap.

03

The language versions and sitemap line lead to errors.

Your pages declare alternate versions at /ua and /de. Both return a server error page. The last line of robots.txt is “Sitemap: https://nuancelasvegas.com/de/sitemap.xml”, which returns “Not Found”. Your working sitemap at /sitemap.xml isn't named there.

04

The homepage is very slow in the load test.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, your homepage's first content appeared at 3.6 seconds and its largest part, the big slider image at the top, at 27.4 seconds. The browser was also tied up for 740 milliseconds, and the page is about 7.5 MB. One run.

05

The Dubai and contact details have loose ends.

“Book in Las Vegas” goes to a booking page on nuance.zenoti.com. “Book in Dubai” goes to your own homepage. The Dubai call link is written “+971 050 425 1022”, with a 0 kept after the country code, and I didn't test it. The Dubai fax number is the same as the Las Vegas fax, (702) 997-1286. None of the 15 pages I saved lists office hours.

06

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

It scored your homepage 79. It found 18 photo links with no readable name, 2 menu buttons with no name, 4 form labels that are hard to read against their background (contrast 2.73), a dropdown with no label, 2 headings out of order and 3 slider dots that are too small to tap.

What's working

Your two doctors and 37 service pages are easy to find from the menu, and the service pages I opened run up to about 1,400 words. The phone (702) 762-3223 is a tap-to-call link on every page I saved, the Las Vegas address (3605 South Town Center Drive, Suite C) is on the homepage and Contact page, three patient reviews are on the homepage with a full Reviews page, and the contact form lets a visitor choose Las Vegas or Dubai. When I searched your name, your homepage was the first result.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether the contact form and the booking page work, and speed on pages other than the homepage. The content of individual blog posts. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt only blocks /admin. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked.

  • Gap
    Titles and descriptions are placeholders or empty

    The homepage title is “Home page” and its description is “ss”. 44 of the 46 loading sitemap pages have an empty title and none has a description.

  • Gap
    Business markup is thin and untyped

    The homepage markup lists a name, image, phone, email and website. It doesn't say what kind of business it is, and has no address, hours or social links.

  • Gap
    Sitemap and robots.txt point at broken addresses

    11 of the 59 sitemap addresses lead to your “Not Found” page, and robots.txt names /de/sitemap.xml, which returns “Not Found”.

  • Gap
    Alternate language versions error out

    The alternate versions your pages declare at /ua and /de return a server error page.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns “not found”. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 16, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
